## About the Archive

The **Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive** is an open-access digital archive dedicated to preserving the history of one of the most significant LGBTQ-affirming independent Catholic communities in the United States. Founded in New York City during a period of profound social and religious change, the Church of the Beloved Disciple provided a sacramental home for LGBTQ Christians and their allies while demonstrating that Catholic tradition and queer identity could flourish together within a vibrant liturgical community.

The archive preserves the surviving documentary record of that community through photographs, newspapers, correspondence, liturgical publications, audio recordings, ephemera, and other historical materials. Together, these sources illuminate the religious, cultural, and social life of a congregation whose history has too often remained absent from broader narratives of American Christianity.

## The Collection

The archive contains hundreds of digitized primary sources dating primarily from the late 1960s through the 1980s, including:

- Photographs documenting worship, processions, community life, and special events
- Church newsletters, bulletins, and liturgical texts
- Newspapers and magazine coverage
- Correspondence and administrative records
- Audio recordings
- Printed ephemera and devotional materials

High-resolution digital images are provided whenever possible to support scholarly research, teaching, and public engagement.

## Research Themes

The collection supports research in a wide range of fields, including:

- LGBTQ religious history
- Independent Catholicism
- Queer theology and spirituality
- American religious history
- Urban history
- Liturgical studies
- Material religion
- Community formation and religious activism

Rather than presenting these materials simply as historical artifacts, the archive documents how one community creatively negotiated questions of identity, authority, ritual, and belonging during a pivotal period in American religious history.

## About the Project

The Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive is directed by **Roy A. Fisher, Ph.D.**, Assistant Professor of Theological Studies at Loyola Marymount University.

Developed through the Fisher Digital Humanities Lab, the project combines archival preservation with digital scholarship to make rare historical materials freely accessible to researchers, students, community members, and the broader public. The archive forms part of an ongoing research initiative examining queer sacramentality, independent Catholic movements, and LGBTQ religious history in twentieth-century America.

## Rights and Citation

Unless otherwise noted, digital images remain subject to copyright or donor agreements. Researchers should consult the rights information associated with individual items before publication or redistribution.

When citing materials from this archive, please include the item title, identifier, and the **Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive** as the source.

## Acknowledgments

This archive exists because individuals preserved these materials over many decades. Their commitment to safeguarding the history of the Church of the Beloved Disciple has made this digital collection possible for future generations of researchers and community members.
