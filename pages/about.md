## About the Archive

The **Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive** is an open-access scholarly digital archive documenting a pioneering LGBTQ-affirming independent Catholic congregation in New York City, active ca. 1968–1986. Drawing from the Church of the Beloved Disciple physical archive, the project brings digitized primary sources together with researched descriptions, metadata, and critical historical interpretation.

The Church of the Beloved Disciple provided a sacramental home for LGBTQ Christians during a period of profound social and religious change. Its documentary record illuminates how members inhabited and shaped Catholic traditions, sustained community, and developed forms of queer spiritual creativity amid religious exclusion and the emerging movements for LGBTQ liberation.

The archive approaches these materials as sources requiring careful interpretation. Selection, description, organization, and contextualization constitute central elements of its scholarly work. Attention to provenance, historical context, conflicting accounts, and gaps in the surviving record supports a critical understanding of the congregation and its place within broader histories of religion and LGBTQ life.

## The Physical Archive and Digital Collection

The digital archive draws from a physical collection of photographs, newspapers, correspondence, liturgical publications, audio recordings, ephemera, and other historical materials. These sources preserve evidence of the congregation’s religious, cultural, and social life, as well as its relationships with other communities and institutions.

The online collection represents a growing selection from this physical archive. Digitization expands access to the materials, while researched metadata and descriptions help situate individual items within the larger collection. The physical originals remain important sources for studying material features and relationships that digital representations may not fully convey.

The digital collection is continually growing as the database is updated with additional items from the physical archive curated by Roy A. Fisher. New materials, researched descriptions, and contextual information are added as digitization and scholarship progress.

Materials represented in the collection include:

- Photographs documenting worship, processions, community life, and special events
- Church newsletters, bulletins, and liturgical texts
- Newspaper and magazine coverage
- Correspondence and administrative records
- Audio recordings
- Printed ephemera and devotional materials

High-resolution digital images are provided whenever possible to support scholarly research, teaching, and public engagement.

## Research Themes and Scholarly Approach

The archive supports research in:

- LGBTQ religious history
- Independent Catholicism
- Queer theology and spirituality
- American religious history
- Urban history
- Liturgical studies
- Material religion
- Community formation and religious activism

Its scholarly approach examines relationships among people, institutions, events, rituals, and objects. Descriptions and contextual commentary explore how members of the Church of the Beloved Disciple negotiated religious authority, sacramental belonging, and communal life within the changing social landscape of twentieth-century America.

A central concern is queer sacramentality: the ways LGBTQ Christians inhabited and shaped sacramental traditions in the face of marginalization and exclusion. The collection supports inquiry into the relationship between religious practice and social transformation, while making primary sources available for further interpretation and independent research.

## About the Project

The Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive is directed by **Roy A. Fisher, Ph.D.**, Assistant Professor of Theological Studies at Loyola Marymount University.

Developed through the Fisher Digital Humanities Lab, the project combines the preservation and digitization of a physical archive with historical research, scholarly description, and public access. Its organization and interpretation of primary sources form part of an ongoing research initiative examining queer sacramentality, independent Catholic movements, and LGBTQ religious history in twentieth-century America.

The archive also provides source material for [*Queer Sacramentality: The Church of the Beloved Disciple*](https://exhibits.lgbtran.org/exhibits/show/cbd), an online exhibition presented by the LGBTQ Religious Archives Network. The exhibition offers a thematic interpretation of selected materials, while the digital archive provides a growing resource for broader exploration, teaching, and research.

## Rights and Citation

The Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive holds the rights to all materials created by Robert Mary Clement and the Church of the Beloved Disciple.

The collection also includes materials created by third parties, such as newspaper and magazine articles, which may remain subject to the rights of their respective creators or publishers. Researchers should consult the rights information associated with individual items and contact the archive regarding permission for publication or reuse where required.

When citing materials from this archive, please include the item title, identifier, and the **Church of the Beloved Disciple Archive** as the source. Include the item’s web address and your date of access where required by your citation style.
